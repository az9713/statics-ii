Statics II: rigorous-explainer audit and revision

Course index · Complete revised textbook · Verification record

This revision applies the Claude rigorous-explainer skill to the existing original textbook. It preserves the five modules, 30 lecture topics, all 55 reading entries, and the original 60 foundation problems. Fifteen new extension problems bring the total to 75.

Verification and its limits

The verification record reports the final counts and checks actually completed. LaTeX is compiled into inline SVG before delivery, and the generated documents are checked for broken anchors, duplicate IDs, malformed SVG, unresolved placeholders, and external runtime dependencies. Original equilibrium checks remain in place, with additional computations for the new inverse and sensitivity problems.

The skill’s non-browser checks are run on the output documents. Checks that inspect dollar-delimited TeX have limited applicability to already compiled SVG; successful MathJax compilation and explicit equation-source checks provide the relevant evidence. A proof-inventory checker confirms adjacency, not the truth of a proof; the derivations were also reviewed mathematically.

Browser layout, responsive and print behavior, animation timing, and browser-level overlap checks remain unverified. Standalone SVG raster inspection checks diagram appearance but does not establish whole-page browser layout.

The skill’s network-dependent template was adapted to preserve the agreed offline requirements: equations remain precompiled, and there is no JavaScript or CDN dependency. A native SVG animation is explicitly labeled as a sequence of static cable shapes, not a dynamic simulation. A static comparison is displayed under reduced-motion and print styles.

What this audit does not claim

This is a mathematical and pedagogical revision of an independently authored textbook. The audit does not establish equivalence to external videos, worksheets, or quizzes and is not independent professional peer review.

The foundation questions remain shorter applications; the new C/D/K extension sets carry the deeper investigations and their own task-specific figures. The no-wall-of-text and exact browser label-clearance requirements remain subject to rendered-page review once browser access is available. These are reported as open verification items, not as passed skill gates.

Reference checks

The beam relationships were checked against the University of Alberta’s load–shear–moment treatment. The belt model was checked against Engineering Statics’ flexible-belt chapter. The linked UBC catenary PDF returned a browser-verification page during this audit; it remains further reading rather than verified source content. The catenary result is derived explicitly and checked numerically in this textbook.

Project maintenance

The public HTML files are directly editable and contain their own styles, diagrams, and compiled equations. Run python tools/package.py to check local links and recreate the downloadable ZIP after editing.
