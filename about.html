Engineering Mechanics: Statics II

An independently authored, AI-assisted textbook for self-study. It develops statics through plain-English explanations, mathematical derivations, worked examples, and engineering diagrams.

Read the course · Complete textbook · Download the offline edition

Contents

Five modules cover equilibrium and multi-body systems, plane trusses, space trusses and internal forces, cables, and friction. The edition contains 30 lecture chapters, 55 reading entries, 75 solved problems, 53 engineering diagrams, and 376 rendered equation occurrences.

Using and editing the course

Download and extract the ZIP, then open index.html in a browser. No installation, server, account, or internet connection is required for the teaching material. External references require internet access. Solutions expand when selected. Browser print controls provide a way to print or save pages as PDF.

The HTML pages are the editable public edition. Styles, SVG diagrams, and compiled equations are embedded. Mathematical source expressions are retained in the equation markup. After edits, run python tools/package.py from the repository root to validate the package and update its ZIP.
